The decision this research informs

AI discovery points to purchase confidence (fit / quality / styling) as the top in-scope opportunity, with price-waiting frequent but off-limits (no-money constraint). These interviews must confirm or overturn that and reveal the root cause + real workarounds.

1 · Who we interview

Screen in only if all true:

  • Shops fashion online; uses Myntra at least monthly
  • Has 10+ items in their Myntra wishlist right now
  • Saved something in the last 30 days they haven't purchased

Quota mix: 3–4 women2–3 menage 20–35≥2 ethnic/occasion buyers≥1 "saves a lot, buys little"

Screener questions

  1. How often do you shop fashion on apps like Myntra? (need monthly+)
  2. Roughly how many items are in your wishlist now? (need 10+)
  3. Last month, did you save something you liked but haven't bought? (need yes)
  4. When you save and don't buy, what usually happens? (context)
  5. Age / gender / city (quota)

2 · Recruiting fast

Own network + one referral hop ("someone who hoards wishlist items"); communities like r/IndianFashionAddicts; college/office groups.

Hi! I'm doing product research on how people shop for fashion online (not selling anything). If you use Myntra and have a pile of saved items you haven't bought, I'd love 30 mins this week — you'd just walk me through your wishlist. Free & casual. Interested?

Offer a convenient slot, not money — keeps it consistent with the no-incentive spirit and avoids biasing answers.

3 · Discussion guide (30–35 min)

Principles: ask about the last real time, not hypotheticals · never say "confidence"/"fit problem" or pitch a solution · ask "why" 3× · let silence breathe · 80% them talking.

A · Warm-up (3m)

  • Tell me about the last fashion thing you actually bought online — what made you finally buy it?
  • How do you use the wishlist/save feature — what goes in there?

B · Wishlist walk-through — the core (15m) · screen-share

Pick 3–4 saved-but-not-bought items. For each:

  • When & why did you save this one?
  • What did you think would happen after saving it?
  • Do you still want it? Honest reason you haven't bought yet?
  • What would need to be true to buy it today?
  • Anything you're unsure about? (fit? quality? how you'd wear it? price? else?)
  • Last time you were unsure like this — what did you do to figure it out?

C · Uncertainty & workarounds (7m)

  • Last time you almost bought something saved but backed out — what stopped you?
  • When the app can't tell you something, where do you go? (reviews, Google, YouTube, friends, store, other apps?)
  • Ever asked someone before buying? Who, and what did you ask?

D · Alternatives & external behaviour (5m)

  • Do you compare saved items against each other or other apps? Show me.
  • What finally tips you from "saved" to "bought"?

E · Close (2m)

  • If a genie removed ONE annoying thing between "I like this" and "I bought it," what would you kill?
  • Anything about your saved items I should've asked but didn't?
