How the engine works (methodology) ▸

This is the operational front-end of a two-stage discovery workflow. Stage 1 (offline, LLM-assisted): qualitative reading of hundreds of public reviews to induce a 10-theme pain-point taxonomy for fashion wishlist abandonment, each theme mapped to a stage of the wishlist→purchase funnel and rated for user impact, build feasibility, and whether it's actionable without money. Stage 2 (this tool): that taxonomy is operationalized as a transparent classifier that tags any volume of pasted feedback, quantifies theme frequency and sentiment, and computes opportunity scores — turning raw text into a ranked, comparable opportunity map.

Classification

Each snippet is matched against per-theme keyword & phrase lexicons (case-insensitive, word-boundary aware). A snippet can carry multiple themes. Theme frequency = share of snippets carrying that theme.

Sentiment

Lexicon polarity per snippet (positive/negative term counts + negation flip), aggregated to a conversation-level split.

Opportunity score

score = (freq ÷ max_freq) × (impact ÷ 3) × (feasibility ÷ 3) × 100, where impact and feasibility (1–3) are the taxonomy priors. Themes marked out-of-scope (price/discount levers) are shown for completeness but excluded from the recommended ranking.

Honesty note

The bundled corpus is paraphrased from public reviews to protect against passing off invented quotes as verbatim; frequencies from it are indicative. For decision-grade numbers, paste a real scraped review set — the engine recomputes everything live in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; all processing is client-side.
